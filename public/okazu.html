Case 01

Office worker, 9–5

Weekday desk job, minimal time to research nutrition.
NoticedBody fat percentage in the obese range on a routine scan; low energy by mid-afternoon.
SelectedHigh body fat %, Low energy / fatigue
PrescribedSaba Shioyaki with Daikon Oroshi, Shogayaki

"I didn't know which food to eat — I just knew I should change something. Having two dishes on rotation was easier than a diet plan I'd never follow."

Case 02

Retail sales manager

Long shifts on her feet, wanted to check nutrition before booking a clinic visit.
NoticedOccasional lightheadedness and fatigue by the end of a shift.
SelectedAnemia / lightheaded, Low energy / fatigue
PrescribedAsari no Miso Shiru, Hourensou no Gomaae

"I wanted to rule out the obvious first. Two weeks of clam miso soup and I felt more like myself — I still went to get checked, but I went in with a habit already started."
